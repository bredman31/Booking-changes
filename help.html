Your booking appears on the calendar

When you open the calendar you'll see all the rooms across the top and time slots down the left side. Each coloured block is an existing booking.

👆Empty white slots are available — click one to book it.
🔀Use Day View or Week View buttons (top right) to switch between views.
⬅️Use the arrows or the date picker at the top to navigate to different days.
🔄Click Refresh Data to see the latest bookings.

What do the colours mean?

Each counsellor is assigned a colour so you can quickly see who has booked which slots. Your own bookings will always appear in the same colour.

Tip: Click the Today button to jump back to today's date at any time.

Click on any empty (white) time slot in the calendar. A booking panel will appear showing the room, date, time and cost.

From here you can:

💬Add an optional comment to your booking.
💳Choose your payment method (see Payment Options below).
🛒Click Add to Basket if you want to book multiple slots before paying.
Tip: If you're booking a Car Park space, you'll need to enter your car registration number in the comments field.

The basket lets you collect several bookings and pay for them all at once. When you add a slot to the basket, it appears as a dark grey block on the calendar so you know it's reserved.

🛒The basket button appears at the bottom right of the screen once you've added an item. Click it to open the basket panel.
❌Click the red × next to any item to remove it from the basket.
🗑️Click Clear All to empty the entire basket.
💰When you're ready, click Pay & Confirm to proceed to payment.
📒Or click Book on Account to add everything to your weekly account (see below).
Tip: If you have wallet credit, the basket will show your available credit at the bottom.

When you pay for a booking (or a basket of bookings), you'll see the Payment Options screen:

Option How it works
Pay with Card Pay immediately via Stripe (secure card payment). You'll be redirected to a payment page and returned to the calendar once complete.
Book on Account Add to your weekly tab. All bookings for the week accumulate and need to be paid by Friday at 6pm. Your running balance is shown on the left side of the calendar.
Use Wallet Pay using credit in your wallet (from cancelled bookings). Only shown if you have credit available.
Use Credit + Pay Remainder If your wallet credit doesn't cover the full amount, this option uses all your credit and charges the rest to your card.
Important: Account bookings must be paid by Friday 6pm each week. If you have an overdue balance from a previous week, you won't be able to book on account until it's cleared.

If you book on account, your current week's balance is shown at the top left of the calendar under your name. The billing week runs from Saturday to Friday.

💰This week — shows your current running total for unpaid bookings this week.
⚠️Overdue balance — if a previous week is unpaid, it will show in red. Click on it to pay.

You can pay your account balance at any time by clicking on the balance amount. You don't have to wait until Friday — paying early is fine!

Tip: When you make a card payment for new bookings, any overdue balance from previous weeks is automatically included, so everything gets settled at once.

If you cancel a paid booking, the refund goes into your wallet as credit rather than back to your card. This credit can be used towards future bookings.

Your wallet balance is displayed in the green box on the left side of the calendar. When you make a booking, if you have wallet credit you'll see additional payment options to use it.

Tip: If your wallet credit is less than the booking cost, you can use the "Use Credit + Pay Remainder" option to combine credit with a card payment.

Click on one of your own bookings in the calendar (the coloured blocks with your name). The left panel will show the booking details with options to cancel or change it.

To cancel: Click the red "Cancel This Appointment" button. If it was a paid booking, the amount will be added to your wallet credit.

To change: Use the dropdowns under "Or Request Changes" to select a new room, date, or time, then click "Submit Change Request".

Same-day and future bookings

When What you can do
Future bookings (tomorrow onwards) Change room, date, or time. Cancel for a wallet refund.
Today's bookings Change room or time only. Cannot cancel or move to a different date.
Important: Same-day bookings cannot be cancelled online. If you need to cancel a booking on the day, please contact the centre directly.

Car park spaces are shown in the last column of the calendar. They work the same as room bookings but with one key difference:

Required: When booking a car park space, you must enter your car registration number in the comments field. The booking cannot be confirmed without it.

The car park column shows how many spaces are available (e.g. "1/4 booked" means 3 spaces remain). Each car park slot corresponds to one space for one hour.

My booking isn't showing: Click the Refresh Data button. New bookings can take a few seconds to appear after payment.

I can't click on a time slot: The slot is already booked by another counsellor. Only empty (white) slots are available.

The page looks blank or won't load: Try opening the link from your email again. Your access link may have expired — contact the centre for a new one.

Payment didn't go through: If your card payment was interrupted, no booking will have been created. Your basket will be restored so you can try again.

I can't book on account: You may have an overdue balance from a previous week. Pay the outstanding amount first, then the account option will be available again.

Any other issues: Please contact the Cherry Tree Centre office and we'll be happy to help.
